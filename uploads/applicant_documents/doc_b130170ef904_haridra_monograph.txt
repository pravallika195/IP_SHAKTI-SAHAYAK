TRADITIONAL FORMULATION MONOGRAPH: HARIDRA EMULGEL

Ingredients:
1. Curcuma longa rhizome powder (Haridra)
2. Maricha (Piper nigrum) fruit extract
Therapeutic indication: Vrana ropana (wound healing) and Twak roga (dermatological conditions).
Preparation Method: Aqueous decoction dispersed in natural hydrophilic polymer base.
